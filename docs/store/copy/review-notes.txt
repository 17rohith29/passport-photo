WHAT THIS APP DOES

Exact ID Photo turns a photo already on the device into a passport or visa photo cropped to the exact size a particular country's document requires. All processing happens on the device.

TARGET AUDIENCE AND THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES

Anyone applying for a passport or a visa. Getting a compliant photo normally means paying a shop, or uploading a photo of your own face to a website. This app measures and crops on the phone, so the photo never leaves it, and it costs nothing.

HOW TO SET UP AND TEST

There is no account, no login, no credentials, no paid content and nothing to configure.

1. Launch the app. It pre-selects a document from the device region and labels it "Our guess. Tap to change." Tap that row to pick from ten documents.
2. Tap "Choose Photo" and select ANY photo containing a clearly visible face from the test device's library. An ordinary portrait or selfie is fine. "Take a Photo" uses the camera instead.
3. The app measures the face, straightens and crops the image, and lists what it could and could not check.
4. Tap Save. The photo is written to the library using add-only Photos permission. A "Saved" screen then explains how to print it.

The photo must contain a face the system can detect. A landscape or an object is refused with an explanation; that is intended behaviour, not a failure.

Also worth exercising:
- "Requirements" lists every rule for the selected document, the official source it came from, and the date it was last verified.
- For the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and Australia a "Keep my own background" toggle appears, because those four rule out software-edited photos. With it on, the app only crops and resizes.
- On the camera screen a "Voice" button speaks the framing cues aloud.

EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS AND PLATFORMS

None. This app uses no external service of any kind.

- No network code. The binary contains no URLSession, no Network framework and no sockets. Nothing is uploaded or downloaded at any point, including analytics or crash data.
- No third-party SDKs or libraries. The project has no remote package dependencies.
- No authentication service, no payment processor, no data provider, no advertising network.
- No cloud service and no third-party AI service. Face measurement and background separation use Apple's on-device Vision framework.

Apple frameworks only: SwiftUI, Vision, Core Image, Core Graphics, AVFoundation, Photos, PhotosUI, ImageIO, UIKit, Foundation.

The source is public under the Apache 2.0 licence at github.com/17rohith29/passport-photo, so every claim above can be verified directly.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES

The app behaves identically in every region. All documents are available everywhere and no feature or content is gated by country. The only region-dependent behaviour is cosmetic: on first launch the app pre-selects a likely document from the device region, clearly marked as a guess, and any other document can be chosen.

REGULATED INDUSTRY AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL

This app is not a government service. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or authorised by any government or passport authority, and it does not claim to be anywhere in the app or in its App Store listing.

It contains no protected third-party material. What it holds are factual measurements, such as photo dimensions in millimetres, head-height ranges and background colour, read from each authority's own published guidance. For every document the app shows the official source and the date it was last verified. Where a figure could not be confirmed first-hand, the app says so rather than presenting it as verified.

The app never claims a photo will be accepted. Every document lists the rules the app cannot check, such as how recently the photo was taken or whether the subject wears glasses, so a passed check is never presented as approval.
