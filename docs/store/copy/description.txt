Turn a photo you already have into a passport or visa photo, cut to the exact size that document requires.

Free. No watermark on the result, no subscription to unlock saving, no account to create. Your photo never leaves the phone, because the app contains no networking code at all.

HOW IT WORKS

Pick the document, choose a photo, and the app measures your face, straightens the picture, crops it to the required millimeter, and tells you what it could and could not check. Then it shows you how to print it.

WHAT IT DOES

• Measures your head from chin to crown and crops so it lands inside the range the document specifies
• Straightens a tilted photo and replaces the background with the required color
• Checks what can be measured: head size, eye line, head angle, sharpness, even lighting, eyes open, mouth closed, and whether the photo is in color
• Says plainly what it cannot check, so a green check is never mistaken for approval
• Gives you one photo for an online upload, or a 4 by 6 inch sheet with cut guides
• Walks you through printing, including how many copies that country wants and where to submit them

DOCUMENTS

Passports and visas for Australia, Canada, China, India, Japan, Russia, the Schengen Area, the United Kingdom and the United States. More are added over time.

Fewer countries than some apps list. Each one here was read from that government's own published guidance, and the app shows you the source and the date it was checked.

IF YOUR COUNTRY DOES NOT ACCEPT EDITED PHOTOS

Australia, Canada, the United Kingdom and the United States all rule out a photo edited with software, and replacing the background counts as editing it. For those four, turn on "Keep my own background" and the app will crop and resize only. It will then tell you honestly whether your own wall is light enough to pass.

WHY YOU CAN BELIEVE THE PRIVACY CLAIM

The app is open source under the Apache 2.0 license at github.com/17rohith29/passport-photo. The claim that it has no networking code is not something you have to take on trust.

WHAT IT CANNOT DO

No app can promise that an authority will accept your photo. This one measures what can be measured and is straight with you about the rest. It cannot see whether you are wearing glasses, how old the photo is, what paper you print on, or whether your expression suits the person behind the counter. Each of those is listed in the app, document by document, so you know what is still yours to get right.

Where a figure could not be confirmed first hand, the app says so instead of pretending.
