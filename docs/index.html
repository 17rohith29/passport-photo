Exact ID Photo

Turn a photo you already have into a passport or visa photo cut to the exact size your country asks for. Everything happens on your iPhone, and nothing is uploaded.

Support

Something wrong, a measurement that looks off, or a country you want added? Open an issue: github.com/17rohith29/passport-photo/issues. Or email 17rohith29@gmail.com.

If you are reporting a photo that was handled badly, please say which document you picked and what the app told you. Do not send the photo itself unless you are happy for it to be seen.

What it can and cannot do

The app measures what can be measured: head height chin to crown, eye line, head angle, sharpness, even lighting, eyes open, mouth closed, and whether the photo is in colour. It cuts the result to the exact millimetre each document requires.

It cannot tell whether you are wearing glasses, how old the photo is, what paper you print it on, or whether your expression suits the person behind the counter. Each document lists those in the app, so a green tick is never mistaken for approval. No app can promise an authority will accept a photo, and this one does not.

Countries

Australia, Canada, China, India, Japan (passport and visa), Russia, the Schengen Area, the United Kingdom, the United States. Each requirement is read from the issuing authority's own published guidance, with the source and date shown in the app. Where a figure could not be confirmed first-hand, the app says so.

Privacy

Nothing is collected. There is no networking code in the app at all, which you can verify yourself in the source. Full privacy policy.
